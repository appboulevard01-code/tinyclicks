Privacy Policy for TinyClicks

Last updated: 24/09/2026

TinyClicks is designed for young children and their parents. We take children's privacy seriously and built the App to require none of it.

Data Collection

TinyClicks does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, or device identifiers. Specifically, the App:

  • Does not require account creation or sign-in
  • Does not connect to the internet or any remote server
  • Does not use analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs
  • Does not display advertising
  • Does not offer in-app purchases
  • Does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, or storage
  • Does not contain external links or the ability to leave the App

All images, audio, and content are bundled with the App and function entirely offline.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Because TinyClicks collects no data whatsoever from any user, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy requirements.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date.
